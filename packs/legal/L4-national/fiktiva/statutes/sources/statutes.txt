FIKTIVA STATUTES (INVENTED) (FICTIONAL, INVENTED FOR TESTS, NOT LAW)

Article 6 Road closures
INVENTED TEXT. A through road may be closed for more than thirty days only by a ministerial road order.

Article 10 Own case
INVENTED TEXT. A resident may bring a complaint about the handling of their own case to the Fiktiva City Ombudsman Desk.

Article 14 Court orders
INVENTED TEXT. A written order from a Fiktiva court that reaches a public body must be passed to its legal office without delay.
